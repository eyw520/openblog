This is a standalone page. It lives at `content/pages/about.md` and is published at `/about`.

Pages are for the writing that is not a post: an about page, a colophon, a page listing what you are doing now. They have no date, they do not appear in any archive, and they stay out of the feed.

Making one is a single file. Drop it in `content/pages/`, give it a `title`, and it exists. Add `nav: true` and it appears in the menu at the top of every page; `navOrder` decides where in that menu it sits, with lower numbers first.
